Questions

FAQ

Is this real car insurance?

No. This is a product prototype only. U Shall Receive is not a licensed insurer or agency, no coverage is bound, and no payment is processed. A real U.S. auto policy must be issued by a licensed insurance carrier and sold by a licensed producer where required.

Can everyone actually get the $300 or $600 six-month prices?

No, and this demo does not promise that. State law generally requires rates to reflect risk — age, driving record, vehicle, location, and coverage all matter. The $300 PL/PD and $600 Full Coverage figures are sample baselines for each plan. The quote calculator then applies a few obvious risk adjustments so you can see how a real rating engine might change a premium; those adjustments are illustrative, not actuarial.

Why do you ask for a ZIP code and driving history?

To make the sample quote feel like a real flow. In production, location and history would feed a licensed carrier’s rating engine and state-required underwriting rules. Here they only change a mock price with labeled add-ons, and the answers never leave your browser.

Will you charge my card?

No. Checkout is simulated. Card fields are disabled placeholders, and the only action is a “Simulate payment” button that does not transmit or store payment data.

Where is my information stored?

Only in this browser’s localStorage, so you can refresh or come back during the demo. Clear site data to remove it. Nothing is sent to a server.

What would it take to offer this for real?

State insurance licenses (or a partnership with a licensed MGA / carrier), an approved rating and underwriting engine, surplus lines or admitted paper depending on the model, compliant payments and escrow, and consumer notices required by each Department of Insurance. This demo is a design exploration of that future product, not a path around those requirements.
